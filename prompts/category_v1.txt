You are classifying risk factors disclosed by US banks in Item 1A of their SEC Form 10-K filings.

Assign each risk factor to exactly one category, based on what the risk is FUNDAMENTALLY about — not on every consequence it happens to mention.

CATEGORIES

financial
    Money. Borrowers failing to repay; loan losses; allowance adequacy; collateral
    values; deposits leaving; funding costs; access to capital markets; interest
    rates; securities and investment valuations; trading; general economic
    conditions affecting the balance sheet.

operational
    Things breaking. Cyber attacks and information security; system failures and
    outages; third-party vendors and service providers; fraud and theft;
    attracting and retaining employees; internal controls; failed technology
    projects; business continuity.

regulatory
    Rules and courts. Laws and legislation; supervisors, examinations and
    enforcement; regulatory capital requirements and stress testing; litigation
    and legal proceedings; fines and penalties; compliance obligations;
    government policy and fiscal action.

strategic
    The plan failing. Competition; acquisitions, mergers and integration; growth
    plans and expansion; falling behind on technology or customer expectations;
    reputation, brand and public perception; ESG positioning.

DECISION RULES

1. Judge primarily from the heading. The heading states what the risk IS. Bodies
   list consequences that spill across every category and will mislead you.

2. Pick what the risk is fundamentally about. A cyber-attack risk that also
   mentions regulatory fines is operational, not regulatory. A competition risk
   that mentions reduced earnings is strategic, not financial.

3. Umbrella macroeconomic risks — "difficult economic conditions may adversely
   affect our business" — are financial, even when the body lists credit,
   operational and strategic consequences.

4. Umbrella climate risks are financial. Climate REGULATION is regulatory.

5. Government shutdowns, fiscal policy and legislative uncertainty are
   regulatory, even where the stated impact is on lending.

6. Every item must receive exactly one category. Do not abstain, do not invent
   categories, and do not return more or fewer items than you were given.

OUTPUT

Return a JSON array. One object per input item, in the same order, each with:
    id       the integer id given in the input
    category one of: financial, operational, regulatory, strategic

Return nothing except the JSON array.
